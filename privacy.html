Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VinPop, operated by AI Business Solutions LLC ("VinPop," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you submit a promotional offer request form provided by VinPop on a participating automotive dealership's website, or request a demo on vinpop.io (together, the "Service").

No selling, no texting: We do not sell your personal information. We do not send text messages based on a promotional offer request, and the offer form does not ask for SMS consent. Your information is used to deliver your request to the dealership whose website you were on.

Information we collect

When you submit a promotional offer request form on a participating dealership's vehicle page, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • The vehicle you were viewing and the offer shown to you (such as the VIN, year, make, model, stock number, and offer amount)
  • Your IP address and browser details

The offer itself is made by the dealership, which is responsible for its terms and for honoring it.

When you request a demo on vinpop.io, we collect the name, dealership, email address, and phone number you enter.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver your promotional offer request, with the vehicle and the offer you requested, to the dealership whose website you were on, so they can follow up with you
  • Respond to demo requests made on vinpop.io
  • Prevent duplicate, automated, or abusive submissions
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

How we share information

We share information only as needed to provide the Service:

  • With the dealership whose website you were on when you submitted a promotional offer request, so they can follow up with you about that vehicle and offer by phone or email.
  • With service providers who help us operate (for example, our email delivery provider and hosting provider), who are permitted to use the information only to perform services for us.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of VinPop, our users, or others.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Your choices

  • You may request access to or deletion of your information by contacting us at the email below.
  • To stop follow-up from a dealership, contact that dealership directly. The dealership controls its own communications with you.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide the Service and to meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do.
