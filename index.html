How VinPop Works

01

Shopper views a vehicle

A shopper lands on one of your vehicle pages and spends time with a specific car. VinPop stays off search and listing pages.

02

VinPop pops a real offer

After a delay you choose, a dollar offer appears on that exact car, in your dealership's name and colors. On desktop it can also appear as the shopper moves to leave.

03

The lead goes to your CRM

Name, email, phone, the vehicle and the offer they claimed are sent to your CRM as soon as they submit. No manual entry.

Questions Dealers Ask

Is VinPop a lead form?

Yes. It asks for a name, email and phone number. The difference is what the shopper gets first: a specific dollar offer on the car they're looking at.

Will it annoy my shoppers?

It's limited on purpose. It appears at most once per vehicle per visit, never on search pages, and not again on that car for a week by default. If someone closes it, a small tab stays at the edge of the page in case they change their mind, and they can close that too. Once a shopper submits, it stops showing.

Can I run different offers on different vehicles?

Yes. Several offers can run at once, each limited by new or used, price range, make and model, with its own dates. If a car qualifies for more than one, the offer ranked highest is the one that shows.

Who sets up the offers?

Your agency sets up and changes offers for you. You see the leads, in your CRM and in a lead list with the offer each shopper claimed.

How do leads get into my CRM?

They're sent in ADF/XML format to your CRM's lead email address, the standard way dealer CRMs take in third-party leads. If a send fails, it's retried automatically and can be resent by hand.

Does VinPop text my shoppers?

No. It doesn't ask for text consent and nothing is texted from it. Your team follows up the way they normally would.

Does the voucher guarantee the offer?

No. The voucher is a record of the shopper's request, and its code identifies the lead. Your dealership verifies it against the lead and honors the offer under your own terms.

What does it take to install?

One Google Tag Manager tag and your inventory feed.
